  • Product Reviewed: Matcha Latte & Matcha Mochie

    Posted by: Trí Beef

    Date: 02/10/2024

    Content:

    I recently tried the matcha latte and matcha mochi, and I would say both items were decent but not exceptional.

    Matcha Latte: The flavor of the matcha latte was good but a bit too mild for my taste. I was hoping for a stronger matcha flavor to come through, but it seemed a bit diluted. The sweetness level was just right, though, and the drink was well-balanced in terms of milk and matcha. However, I think it could benefit from a bolder matcha presence.

    Matcha Mochi: The mochi had a nice chewy texture, but the matcha filling was also on the lighter side. While the mochi itself was fresh and soft, the matcha flavor didn’t quite stand out. I was expecting a richer matcha taste, similar to traditional matcha desserts.

  • Diabetic-Friendly Cookie Recipe

    Posted by: Jimin

    Date: 22/09/2024

    Content:

    Hello everyone! I’d like to share a delicious cookie recipe that’s perfect for those with diabetes. These cookies are low in sugar and high in flavor, making them a great treat without the guilt.

    Ingredients: 1 cup almond flour; 1/4 cup coconut flour; 1/2 teaspoon baking powder; 1/4 teaspoon salt; 1/2 cup unsweetened applesauce (acts as a natural sweetener); 1/4 cup erythritol (or your preferred sugar substitute); 1 teaspoon vanilla extract; 1/2 cup sugar-free chocolate chips (optional)

    Instructions: Preheat the Oven: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Mix Dry Ingredients: In a bowl, combine almond flour, coconut flour, baking powder, and salt. Stir well to mix. Combine Wet Ingredients: In another bowl, mix the applesauce, erythritol, and vanilla extract until smooth. Combine Mixtures: Gradually add the dry mixture to the wet mixture, stirring until a dough forms. If you’re using chocolate chips, fold them in at this stage. Shape Cookies: Using a tablespoon, scoop out the dough and place it on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Flatten each scoop slightly with the back of a spoon. Bake: Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the edges are golden. Let them cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
